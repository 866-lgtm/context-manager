- `ContextStrategy.detach?()`, a new optional member: the manager is done with the strategy and the strategy lets go of what it holds in the process. `ContextManager.close()` calls it, whether or not the manager owns its store, and `setStrategy()` calls it on the strategy it replaces before initializing the new one. A later `setStrategy()` of the same instance initializes it again. `AutobiographicalStrategy` uses it to leave its compression lane, so a pause it holds, which nothing will lift once it's detached, stops counting toward the lane's stall clock.
