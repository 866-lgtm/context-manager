- With `imageStripHysteresisRatio` set, a `previewContext` call, or the select a dry compile runs, advances the image watermark for its own render only. It no longer writes the watermark or leaves it advanced, so a preview with stricter image limits can't change which images the next live compile strips, and a preview that changes only the ratio prices its images for that ratio. `getRenderStats` before the first compile also reconstructs its window without changing or persisting the watermark or retaining the estimates priced under that temporary trim. Releasing a compression hold whose deadline has passed, which a dry compile does before its select, still commits as before, including the watermark advance made by the release's rebuild; the following dry select leaves that state unchanged.
