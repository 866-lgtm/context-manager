- A quarantined merge no longer freezes the whole summary pyramid. Merge candidate selection always offered the oldest contiguous run; when that exact group was quarantined, `enqueueMerge` silently declined it on every pass and no later group was ever considered (observed: one refused L2 merge left 322 newer L1s unmerged for 16 days, with zero merge calls). While a quarantined merge's group could still be retried as it stands, its sources are now left out of the merge candidates, so they split the frontier like a hole: the summaries on each side merge as usual, a run that ends at the hole merges at two, as one between two summaries does, and no summary above crosses it. A record holds nothing once its group can't be retried as it stands (a source gone or parented, or the group no longer a valid merge), nor for a topology refusal: its sources merge in valid groups, and its own group, offered once it is a valid merge again, queues and the record goes. `clearMergeQuarantine` queues a held group again, unless one of its sources is still held or already queued. A group cleared after merges formed around it merges into one summary, which stays below its neighbours' level when nothing beside it was left unmerged. A store whose merging had stopped this way merges its backlog after the upgrade. The merge-quarantine klaxon's line now counts the records that hold their sources.
- Merge eligibility is enforced in one place for every scheduler: `enqueueMerge` refuses any group containing a held or quarantined source (previously only an exact quarantined set). The adaptive produce path (`enqueueMergeForRange`, which the greedy solvers and kv-unified's latent demand use) also leaves such sources out and takes the longest run beside them; before this it re-requested the exact quarantined group on every pick, so the freeze above still occurred on stores using it. A queued merge holding such a source, as a queue from an older version can, is skipped when it reaches the head, and its other summaries are offered at once.
- Stale merge-quarantine records (sources merged or repaired while the store was closed) are swept at load, not only when the alarm fires.
- `getCompressionDebt()` reports `mergeQuarantineKeys` and `mergeHeldIds`.
