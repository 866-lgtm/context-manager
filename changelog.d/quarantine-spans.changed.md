- The compression-quarantine alarm and `getCompressionQuarantineStatus()` name each quarantined span once. A span can hold one quarantine record per request shape it exhausted, up to three, so the alarm's count of records overstated how many spans were stuck, and its keys didn't say which messages. The status gains `spans`, the longest waiting first: each span's first and last message ids, its age since its chunk closed (from its messages' timestamps, so a re-cut chunk keeps its age), when its earliest record was made, its records, the requests they hold an answer for, and the newest record's last outcome (such as `refusal` or `provider_error:invalid_request`). The alarm line starts with the number of spans, gives the number of records beside it, and lists each span with those figures, its age as "closed 2.3 h ago": the time since its chunk closed, which can be longer than the time since it was first quarantined. `count` and `keys` are unchanged.
