- A strategy that `setStrategy()` replaced, or whose manager closed, no longer goes on compressing (#209). `AutobiographicalStrategy` used to finish a compression in flight and write its summary and chunk record into a store and namespace another strategy now owns, and its background drain went on calling the provider. Now a call already waiting on the provider finishes, with any retries the provider client makes of it, and its result is thrown away, and the drain starts no further call.
- `AutobiographicalStrategy`'s background drain no longer throws from its timer when the store's branch moved between two of its ticks, as with a direct Chronicle branch switch: the drain stops there. It threw "requires reinitialization for the current branch generation" inside a timer callback, where no caller could catch it.
- A drain that is running when the manager's own `switchBranch()` re-initializes `AutobiographicalStrategy` stops at its timer, and the next message starts the new branch's drain.
- An `AutobiographicalStrategy` instance set again while one of its compressions or merges was still waiting on the provider, by `setStrategy()` with the same instance, a manager's `switchBranch()`, or a new manager after `close()`, no longer starts more work when that earlier call settles. Its settling cleared the marker the new generation's tick had set, so the next tick could start work beside the new generation's, such as a second merge of the same sources, writing two summaries over them.
- A merge whose sources were parented before or while its call was out, by another instance sharing the store or in this one, now writes nothing, since a summary has one parent. It checked only its own instance's summaries, and only when it started. An instance whose merge finds another instance's merge of its sources takes that merge up, as it does another instance's L1, rather than choosing the run again.
