- `OverBudgetError.clearedBy` names what clears an over-budget refusal besides a larger budget, which clears any of them: `'compression'` while the strategy still has summaries to produce or merge for this context, `'newer-message'` when the newest message alone is over (windowed passthrough), and `'smaller-recent-window'` when the raw recent window is what's over and the newest message fits. It's empty when nothing else is known to clear the refusal. A host can now tell whether waiting for compression helps without reading the message. The list's type is exported as `OverBudgetClearedBy`; the messages are unchanged.
