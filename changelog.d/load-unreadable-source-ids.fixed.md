- A store holding a summary whose `sourceIds` isn't a list of ids (null, a string, an empty list, or a list holding something other than ids) no longer throws on every compile under adaptive resolution. Such summaries are dropped when the store is opened, as empty ones already were, and what pointed at them is repaired the same way: their children can merge again, a chunk that pointed at a dropped L1 can be compressed again, and a queued merge naming one is removed. A queued merge whose `sourceIds` isn't a list is dropped too.
