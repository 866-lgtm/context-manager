- **Hosts that use a `ContextManager` after calling its `close()`:** `close()` is now final, whoever owns the store. Every operation that writes messages, branches or receipts, or calls into the strategy, throws "ContextManager.<operation> after close()". Among them are `compile()`, `tick()`, `addMessage()`, `setStrategy()`, `switchBranch()`, `acceptRound()`, and the strategy's reads and settings, such as `getSummary()` and `updateHotContextSettings()`. A `setStrategy()`, `switchBranch()` or `fork()` still initializing the strategy when `close()` runs rejects. `isClosed()` answers true after `close()`, where with an app-owned store it answered for the store, and a second `close()` detaches nothing more. With an app-owned store, `close()` used to leave the manager working.
  - **Migration:** open a new manager on the store to go on with it, opening a store the manager owned again by its path. The same strategy instance can be given to the new manager.
  - **Unchanged:** a store the manager owns is closed, and an app-owned store stays open for the app to close. `releaseCompression()` still doesn't throw, reads of messages, branches and receipts still read the store while it's open, and the manager's own setters, such as `setSystemPrompt()`, still answer.
