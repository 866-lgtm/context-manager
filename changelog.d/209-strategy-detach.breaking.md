- **Hosts that use a `ContextManager` after calling its `close()`:** `close()` is now final, whoever owns the store. Every operation that writes messages, branches or receipts, or calls into the strategy, such as `compile()`, `tick()`, `addMessage()`, `setStrategy()`, `switchBranch()` and `acceptRound()`, throws "ContextManager.<operation> after close()". A `setStrategy()`, `switchBranch()` or `fork()` still initializing the strategy when `close()` runs rejects. `isClosed()` answers true, and a second `close()` does nothing. With an app-owned store, `close()` used to leave the manager working.
  - **Migration:** open a new manager on the store to go on with it. The same strategy instance can be given to the new manager.
  - **Unchanged:** a store the manager owns is closed, and an app-owned store stays open for the app to close. `releaseCompression()` still doesn't throw, reads of messages, branches and receipts still read the store while it's open, and the manager's own setters, such as `setSystemPrompt()`, still answer.
